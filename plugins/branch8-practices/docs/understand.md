# Stage 1: Understand the requirement

Record status: `understanding`.

The RD usually picks the ticket before reading it, so they cannot explain it
yet. What this stage guards against is the RD's understanding being shaped by
your reading before they have one of their own.

1. **Lay out the source, without interpretation.** Gather the ticket
   description, attachments and any linked PRD, and present them in a readable
   form. For a long PRD, give only a table of contents with where each part is,
   so the RD knows where to look. Do not say what you think it is for.
   - A link or attachment you cannot open (no connector, no access): say which
     one and ask for it to be pasted or connected. Never summarise or guess
     content you have not read.
2. **The RD restates it.** Ask them, in their own words: what it solves, for
   whom, and what "done" looks like. Incomplete is fine - their version first.
3. **Then yours.** Add what you read, and point out where the two versions
   differ. The differences are where the discussion starts; they often expose
   what the PRD leaves unclear, which carries into stage 2.

"Just tell me" from the RD skips step 2: give your reading, and note in the
record's Purpose section that this part was interpreted by Claude first.

Write the agreed purpose into the record (RD's version, your additions, the
differences), set status `reviewing`, and read `review.md` (next to this file).
